About Novawerke

BUILT AROUND A
SIMPLE QUESTION.

As organizations depend more heavily on AI, what happens when the infrastructure supporting that intelligence is no longer continuously available?

Company

RESILIENT AI INFRASTRUCTURE

Novawerke AI is developing infrastructure intended to help organizations maintain useful AI capability through changing connectivity conditions.

Our current commercial work is focused on learning where that problem is sufficiently painful, costly, or consequential to justify a different approach.

How We Work

LEARN BEFORE WE ASSUME.

We are deliberately pressure-testing the problem with operators, technical leaders, and organizations that use AI in real environments. The objective is not to force a predetermined solution into the market. It is to understand where continuity actually matters and build accordingly.
