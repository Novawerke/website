The problem

AI IS BECOMING INFRASTRUCTURE.
ITS DEPENDENCIES ARE BECOMING RISK.

Today’s AI increasingly assumes reliable access to centralized cloud services, network connectivity, compute, and third-party infrastructure. In practice, networks fail, communications become constrained, bandwidth disappears, and access is interrupted.

We believe AI should continue to work anyway.
